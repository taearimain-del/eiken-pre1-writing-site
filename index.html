意見論述の型（120〜150語・4段落）

序論I believe (do not believe) that ~. I have two reasons for this: ○○ and ○○.

本文1First, 主張 → 説明・具体例 → 結果.

本文2Second, 主張 → 説明・具体例 → 結果.

結論In conclusion, because of ○○ and ○○, I believe that ~.

要約の手順（200語 → 60〜70語）

1. 各段落の「主役の1文」に印をつける（導入 → 利点 → However以降で問題点、の3段落構成が多い）

2. 本文の語句をそのまま写さず、自分の言葉に言い換える

3. 固有名詞・数字・具体例は思い切って削る

4. However / Therefore / In addition などで論理関係を明示して60〜70語にまとめる

ストック表現

  • It is undeniable that ~
  • There is no doubt that ~
  • ~ plays a vital role in …
  • For example, / In fact,
  • As a result, / This leads to ~
  • This is mainly because ~
  • While some argue that ~, I still think ~
  • In addition, / Furthermore,
  • important → essential / crucial
  • many people → a growing number of people
  • problem → drawback / concern
  • companies → businesses / firms
